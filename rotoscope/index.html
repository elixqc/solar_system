02 — Workflow

Procedure.

The step-by-step process of isolating subjects and compositing the final animated piece.

Step 01

Source Video Selection

Identified and acquired the live-action footage to be used as the base layer. Reviewed frame composition and subject movement for rotoscoping feasibility.

Step 02

Frame-by-Frame Tracing

Used rotoscoping tools to manually trace the subject outline across each frame, creating a clean mask that isolates the live-action subject from the background.

Step 03

Layer Compositing

Combined the masked live-action layer with animated and illustrated elements, ensuring proper depth ordering, blending modes, and timing alignment.

Step 04

Audio Integration

Synced background music and sound effects to the visual timeline. Balanced audio levels to complement the rotoscoped motion and pacing of the final video.

Step 05

Text & Typography

Added animated text overlays including titles, subtitles, and motion-tracked labels. Ensured readability and stylistic consistency across all frames.

Step 06

Export & Review

Rendered the final composition at full resolution. Reviewed playback for frame consistency, audio sync, and visual quality before final submission.

03 — Documentation

Weekly Progress.

Week 1

Dancer Rotoscope Start

Started the dancer rotoscope project by preparing the footage and tracing the first frames to establish clean shape and movement direction.

Week 2

Frame-by-Frame Tracing

Continued rotoscoping the dancer with focus on consistent outlines, body proportions, and smooth transition between poses.

Week 3

Motion Cleanup

Refined traced frames and corrected edge jitter to improve motion flow as the dancer moves through faster sequences.

Week 4

Detail Pass

Added details to limbs, clothing, and silhouette depth so the dancer's motion reads clearly in every key pose.

Week 5

Continuing Dancer Rotoscope

Extended frame-by-frame tracing across the next sequence and aligned timing so movement rhythm stays natural.

Week 6

Refinement and Consistency

Adjusted frame consistency across cuts, cleaned stray line artifacts, and maintained dancer proportions from start to end.

Week 7

Middle Sequence Rotoscope

Finished the middle section of the dancer sequence and corrected transitions between turns, spins, and directional changes.

Week 8

Action Segment Tracing

Focused on dynamic movement sections with careful redraws to preserve clarity during rapid dancer motion.

Week 9

Advanced Cleanup

Performed another cleanup pass and removed visual inconsistencies so the dancer rotoscope looks stable across the timeline.

Week 10

Near-Complete Rotoscope

Completed most of the dancer rotoscope sequence and reviewed pacing before moving to the final remaining shots.

Week 11

Dancer Rotoscope Complete

Wrapped up the dancer rotoscope work and prepared the full sequence for text integration and final composition.

Week 12

Lyrics Integration

Added lyrics to the video and synced text timing with the beat and dancer motion to improve storytelling and flow.

Week 13

Finalizing Video

Finalized the full project by polishing transitions, reviewing audio sync, and exporting the finished rotoscope video output.
